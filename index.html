Luiz P. Gressmann

B.Sc. Physics student @ Goethe University Frankfurt

Im a physics student interested in theoretical astrophysics, quantitative finance, factor investing and computational methods in data-driven modeling. Im particularly interested in compact objetcs such as neutron stars and intend to pursue research in this area during my bachelors thesis.

Alongside my studies, I work on a personal project dealing with factor investing, where I intend to optimize the allocation of research-based equity factors in a privately investable portfolio through exchange-traded funds. The project is still in its early stages, but I managed to gain first results and build python code for rolling-window backtestings of different allocation strategies. I intend to continue working on this project and publish my results in the future.
